---
title: "Claude.ai (web & mobile)"
description: "Add CCPEDIA as a custom connector on claude.ai, no desktop app or CLI needed."
---

<Steps>
  ### Open connector settings

    Customize, then Connectors, then the + button, then Add custom connector.

  ### Enter the endpoint

    URL: `https://ccpedia.xyz/mcp`
    Leave the Advanced settings OAuth fields empty. CCPEDIA needs no authentication.

  ### Use it

    Enable the CCPEDIA connector in a chat, then ask a Canton question. Works the same on web and mobile.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">Available on Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. The Free plan is limited to one custom connector in total, not just one for CCPEDIA.</Callout>
